# Luma 0.3.7 Beta

Secondary users no longer need Share System Notifications. Their registered phone's live, trusted Bluetooth bond and resolved services establish presence; Luma never subscribes to, fetches or displays their phone notifications. Existing secondary profiles, Google links and sharing choices are retained. The primary phone still requires its existing notification authorization.

Arrival announcements now play three quick strikes of the same soft local bell. Ordinary primary notifications, time-to-leave reminders and departure announcements remain one strike. Arrivals that happen together share one cue. Existing volume, mute, sleep and alarm/update priorities remain unchanged. The five-second timer alarm is unchanged.

## Install and check

Use Settings → Luma software → Check for updates → review **0.3.7 Beta** → Install. Keep power connected through the verified reboot, then confirm version 0.3.7. No reflash, account reset or new pairing is required.

For a secondary iPhone, leave Share System Notifications off, connect its existing bond, and confirm its nickname and approved calendar/tasks appear. Send that phone a notification: no content or notification chime should appear on Luma. Disconnect it and confirm its private data and remote lock immediately; reconnect and confirm return. Check that the primary's notification permission is still required and its normal notifications remain functional.

After an ordinary range return, listen for the three quick arrival bell strikes; a primary phone notification should ring once. Sleep and mute should still suppress these cues. Real iOS background reconnection and speaker sound need this owner check: the update retains automatic retry attempts but cannot force an unavailable iPhone to reconnect.

This application update preserves settings, accounts, phone bonds, browser enrollments and game saves. USB cooling retains 70°C on / 60°C off, without periodic discovery windows. No OS, firmware, voice model or schema change is included.
