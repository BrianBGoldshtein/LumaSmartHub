# Beta — Luma 0.3.4

Luma 0.3.4 replaces the electronic two-note notification cue with a soft, original bell. A gentle strike rings once and fades naturally over 1.2 seconds. Layered resonances and quicker decay of the bright tones give it a warmer character instead of two plain beeps. It is generated locally, not copied from Apple’s sound files, and requires no new library or downloaded audio asset.

The bell is used by existing notification, time-to-leave and arrival/departure cues. Saved notification volume and enablement, primary permissions, at-most-once playback and existing quiet/privacy rules remain unchanged. Timer alarms and spoken replies are separate and unchanged. All 0.3.3 temperature features are included; the already-published 0.3.3 signed artifact is not replaced.

## Install and listen

Use Settings → Luma software → Check for updates → review 0.3.4 → Install. A primary iPhone remote can also use Software. If still on 0.3.1 or earlier, initiate from wall Settings for this migration. Keep power connected through installation and graceful reboot. Do not reflash the card or reset accounts.

After verifying version 0.3.4, listen to a new notification or time-to-leave cue while your phone is authorized, the display is awake and notification sound is enabled. Adjust Chime volume in Settings if needed. The real speaker and your room determine the final timbre and loudness; mathematical signal checks do not substitute for listening on the Pi.
